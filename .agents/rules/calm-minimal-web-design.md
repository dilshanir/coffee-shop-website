# Coffee Shop Website: Project Details

Follow the Global "Calm, Minimal, Unique Web Design Rule" for all design decisions. This rule adds the project-specific details.

## About the business
- Name: Mandora
- Location: 5th Floor, IBM Building, No. 48 Nawam Mawatha, Colombo 02, Sri Lanka
- Opening hours: Mon–Sun, 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM
- Contact: 0112456783
- Personality: calm, cozy, slow-living. A place to pause and breathe.

## Brand
- Colors: warm coffee tones such as cream, latte beige, soft brown, muted espresso. Use one gentle accent (e.g. sage green or terracotta).
- Mood words: warm, slow, handcrafted, peaceful, inviting.
- Tone of text: friendly, short, poetic but simple. No sales-pushy language.

## Pages / sections (single page, scroll story)
1. Welcome: a calm, signature opening (e.g. steam gently rising from a cup).
2. Our story: how the shop started and what makes it special.
3. Menu: coffee, tea, food, with prices in [LKR].
4. The experience: the space, ambience, gallery of photos.
5. Visit us: map, opening hours, contact.
6. Warm closing: a memorable goodbye message.

## Creative ideas to consider
- A coffee "pour" scroll progress bar (cup fills as the user scrolls).
- A hidden easter egg (e.g. clicking a coffee bean reveals a daily coffee quote).
- A time-of-day greeting: "Good morning, coffee is ready" / "Good evening, time for a slow cup".
- Returning visitors see "Welcome back, your usual?" (localStorage).
